LOOP SECURE USB SETUP

Extract this customer ZIP into its own folder, connect ONE eligible phone by USB, keep the phone unlocked and online, then double-click START-HERE.cmd.

The helper finds or downloads official stable Google Platform Tools automatically and remembers the location. Do not copy files into an SDK folder or type CMD commands. Google tools are downloaded over HTTPS and verified against Google's published checksum. Read the Google tool terms at https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools before use.

Android still requires USB debugging and approval of this laptop on the phone. A phone with existing accounts or another device owner may be ineligible. The helper never removes accounts or resets a phone.

For a new customer, the helper verifies actual Android ownership and starts the administrator-recorded customer connection automatically. Existing customer accounts are preserved; their enrollment code is not consumed or substituted. Keep internet connected and verify the customer name and phone report in the dashboard. Installation alone is not proof of customer enrollment or working restrictions.

This ZIP contains a private one-time code for the intended customer, valid for 30 minutes after generation. Do not mix files between customers or share the ZIP. Request a fresh code only if it expired; repeated downloads do not repair a phone eligibility error.

Already enrolled phones can also use the code-free client update ZIP. Its START-HERE.cmd runs an in-place update and retains the customer connection.

Windows may ask to approve the downloaded script. Android debugging approval and any Android eligibility requirements cannot be bypassed by a one-click helper. If setup stops, read Ownership-check.txt and Enrollment-error.txt; preserve the installed app data.

After enrollment, perform actual handset banking, Wi-Fi/settings, emergency, lock/unlock and restart tests. Once those tests pass, turn off USB debugging and revoke the laptop authorization when it is no longer needed.
